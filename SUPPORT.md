# Getting help

| You need | Go to |
|---|---|
| How to do something | [Q&A in the community](https://github.com/orgs/bastionhelix/discussions/categories/q-a) |
| To suggest a feature | [Ideas in the community](https://github.com/orgs/bastionhelix/discussions/categories/ideas) |
| Help with something private (your site, your data, billing) | Email **support@bastionhelix.com** |
| Setup guides and documentation | [bastionhelix.com/docs](https://bastionhelix.com/docs) |

Email support aims to reply within two business days (Monday to Friday, India time). Details are at [bastionhelix.com/support](https://bastionhelix.com/support).

Please never post customer data, site URLs, tokens or passwords in public discussions.
