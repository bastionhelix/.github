# Code of conduct

The Bastion Helix Community is a place to ask, help and share. To keep it useful for everyone:

**Do**
- Be kind and patient. Everyone was new once.
- Stay on topic: Bastion Helix apps and the work they support.
- Give enough detail for others to help, and mark the reply that solved your question.
- Assume good intent, and disagree with ideas, not people.

**Don't**
- Harass, insult or demean anyone, for any reason.
- Post spam, advertising, or links to unrelated products.
- Share anything private: customer data, site URLs, access tokens or passwords, yours or anyone else's.
- Post other people's personal information.

**If something's wrong**
Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the post, or email support@bastionhelix.com. Moderators may edit, hide or remove posts that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

These rules apply to every space Bastion Helix runs on GitHub.
