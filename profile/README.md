### What we build

Bastion Helix makes apps and tools for the platforms teams already use, built to run inside those platforms instead of beside them.

| Product | What it does | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **[Bastion Helix for Jira](https://bastionhelix.com)** | Charts and dashboards, time tracking, budgets with earned value, diagrams, workflow automation, email-to-issue and AI spend, in one app that runs on Atlassian Forge | Coming to the Atlassian Marketplace |

More apps are on the way.

### How we work
- **Your data stays where it is.** Our Jira app is built on Atlassian Forge, so your data is computed and stored inside Atlassian.
- **What we say is what ships.** Every claim on our site is checked against the product before it goes up.
- **Real people answer.** Ask in the [community](https://github.com/orgs/bastionhelix/discussions), or email support@bastionhelix.com for anything private.
